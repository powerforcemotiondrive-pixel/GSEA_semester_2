Grammar Point: Subject-Verb Agreement & TOEIC Practice

Learn how to find the real subject of a sentence when other words and extra information stand between the subject and the verb.

Matching Subjects & Verbs B1–B2 Grammar Focus
Basic Rule
Main Subject [Singular] + (words in between) + Verb with -s [Singular]

Core Rules: A singular subject takes a singular verb (ending in -s). A plural subject takes a plural verb (without -s). Watch out for words in between:

  • 1. Words in Between (The "of / in / with" trap): In "The integration of light-rail corridors", the real subject is integration (singular), not corridors (plural). The verb must be singular (reduces).
  • 2. Companies & Organizations: Words like the committee, the transit board, or the municipality count as one single unit and take a singular verb.
  • 3. Words like Each / Every / One of: Phrases like Each of the hubs or One of the passengers always take a singular verb.
⚠️ TOEIC Trap Alert: The test often puts plural nouns right in front of the blank (e.g., "...corridors into hubs [ _____ ]") to fool you. Ignore the words inside "of..." or "in..." and match the verb to the very first main noun!
Urban Mobility Examples
✓ "The expansion of electric tramways into suburban districts has improved regional air quality."
✓ "Each of the city's shared mobility hubs provides free solar battery recharging."
TOEIC Diagnostic Practice Part 5: Incomplete Sentences
Sample Item • Reading Section Timed Pace: ~30s

The integration of autonomous light-rail corridors into regional transit hubs ________ commuter congestion by nearly forty percent.

(A) reduces
(B) reduce
(C) reducing
(D) are reducing
💡 Click to Reveal Answer & TOEIC Strategy Analysis ▼
Correct Answer: (A) reduces

Why (A) is correct: The real subject of this sentence is the singular noun The integration. The plural nouns that follow (corridors, hubs) are part of extra descriptions starting with of and into. Because The integration is singular (it), the present tense verb needs an -s (reduces).

Why the Other Choices Are Incorrect:
  • (B) reduce: Plural verb; incorrectly matches the plural words corridors or hubs.
  • (C) reducing: Has an -ing ending; cannot be the main verb without is or are.
  • (D) are reducing: Plural form; does not match the singular subject The integration.
📌 Quick Test Tip: Cross out phrases starting with of, in, or for before picking your verb. Match the verb to the noun that came before them!
