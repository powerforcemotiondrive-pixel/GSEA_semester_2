Session 2: Aircraft Anatomy: Structure & Systems

Master the 18 key vocabulary terms for Aircraft Anatomy and Systems to describe airframe structures, control surfaces, flight deck instruments, and pre-flight walkaround defect reports with technical precision and spoken fluency. Click any term to hear its pronunciation.

Fuselage

01

The central main body structure of an aircraft holding passengers, flight crew, and cargo.

Empennage

02

The tail assembly of an aircraft, consisting of the vertical fin, rudder, horizontal stabilizer, and elevators.

Leading Edge

03

The forwardmost edge of an airfoil or wing that first meets the relative airflow.

Trailing Edge

04

The aft rear edge of an airfoil or wing where airflow passing over and under meets.

Slats

05

Movable aerodynamic surfaces on the leading edge that extend to delay stall at high angles of attack.

Flaps

06

High-lift hinged surfaces on the trailing edge deployed to increase wing surface area and camber during takeoff and landing.

Spoiler (Speed Brake)

07

Hinged plates on the upper wing surface that deploy upward to spoil lift and slow the aircraft.

Horizontal Stabilizer

08

The fixed or trimmable horizontal tail surface providing longitudinal stability in pitch.

Vertical Fin

09

The vertical tail surface that provides directional stability against yaw.

Landing Gear

10

The undercarriage assembly (wheels, oleo struts, brakes) supporting the aircraft on the ground.

Radome

11

The streamlined fiberglass nose cone protecting the weather radar antenna from aerodynamic weather.

Pitot Tube

12

A forward-facing tube mounted on the airframe that measures dynamic ram air pressure for the airspeed indicator.

Static Port

13

A flush-mounted opening on the fuselage side that senses ambient atmospheric pressure for altimeters and VSI.

Sidestick

14

The cockpit hand control stick mounted on the side console used by pilots to send electronic roll and pitch inputs.

Throttle Quadrant

15

The center cockpit console containing engine thrust levers and reverse thrust triggers.

Flight Deck

16

The cockpit area at the front of the aircraft where the flight crew operates the flight controls and instruments.

Galley

17

The aircraft onboard kitchen area where flight attendants prepare passenger meals and store service carts.

Bulkhead

18

A structural vertical dividing wall separating passenger cabins, galleys, or pressurized zones.
