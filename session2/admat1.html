Grammar Point: The Past Perfect Tense & TOEIC Practice

Learn how to use the Past Perfect (had + past participle) to show which past action happened first in TOEIC Part 5.

The "Past Before the Past" B1–B2 Grammar Focus
Sentence Pattern
Subject + had + Past Participle (V3)

When to Use It: Use the Past Perfect when you talk about two events in the past and want to make clear which one happened first:

  • 1. Two past events in order: The earlier event uses had + past participle (*had dominated*), while the later event uses the Past Simple (*entered service*).
  • 2. Finished before a past deadline: Look for time connectors like by the time, before, already, and until (*"By the time the inspectors arrived, the crew had already fixed the issue"*).
⚠️ Past vs. Present Perfect Trap: If a sentence already talks about a past moment (like By the time they arrived...), you cannot use has / have. You must look backward into the past with had + past participle!
Transport Evolution Examples
✓ "By the time commercial jetliners entered service in the late 1950s, transcontinental rail networks had dominated international passenger travel for half a century."
✓ "The regional carrier had already phased out its older turboprop fleet before new carbon regulations took effect."
TOEIC Diagnostic Practice Part 5: Incomplete Sentences
Sample Item • Reading Section Timed Pace: ~30s

By the time the civil aviation board published its updated sustainability directives, the airport authority ________ several energy-saving modifications across its terminals.

(A) implements
(B) had implemented
(C) will implement
(D) has implemented
💡 Click to Reveal Answer & TOEIC Strategy Analysis ▼
Correct Answer: (B) had implemented

Grammar Analysis: The sentence begins with "By the time the board published..." (a finished event in the past). Because the airport authority finished the modifications before that publication happened, you need the Past Perfect: had + past participle (had implemented).

Why the Other Choices Are Incorrect:
  • (A) implements: Present simple; does not fit a situation set in the past.
  • (C) will implement: Future tense; cannot describe something that happened before a past event.
  • (D) has implemented: Present perfect; connects to today, but this entire situation happened in the past.
📌 TOEIC Strategy Rule: Sentences opening with "By the time + [Past Simple clause]" almost always require the Past Perfect (had + past participle) in the main clause.
