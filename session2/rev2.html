Session 2: Sustainable Cities

Review the 18 key terms for Sustainable Cities to build your fluency in discussions, professional reading, and TOEIC practice.

15-Minute City

01

An urban design concept where daily necessities and transport are reachable within a 15-minute walk or cycle.

Urban Heat Island (UHI)

02

Metropolitan areas that experience significantly higher temperatures than surrounding rural zones due to concrete absorption.

Bus Rapid Transit (BRT)

03

A high-capacity bus network operating in dedicated right-of-way lanes with off-board fare collection and station platforms.

Congestion Pricing

04

A surcharge levied on vehicles entering central urban business zones during peak travel hours to reduce traffic.

Permeable Pavement

05

Paving materials that allow stormwater to infiltrate into underlying soil, mitigating urban flash flooding.

Transit-Oriented Development (TOD)

06

Compact urban development clustering housing, commercial spaces, and civic services around rapid transit nodes.

Last-Mile Logistics

07

The final stage of package delivery from a local distribution hub to the consumer doorstep, often carbon-intensive.

Low-Emission District

08

A city sector where combustion engines are prohibited in favor of zero-emission electric vehicles and active mobility.

Urban Metabolism

09

The flow of resources (energy, water, food) consumed by a city and the management of its waste, aiming for circular and sustainable urban systems.

Micro-Consolidation Hub

10

Central neighborhood facilities where parcel freight is sorted and transferred onto electric cargo bikes for zero-emission last-mile delivery.

Pedestrianization

11

Converting vehicular roadways into car-free public zones dedicated exclusively to pedestrians and community life.

Shared Mobility Scheme

12

Public access networks offering shared bicycles, electric mopeds, or car-sharing services on a flexible rental basis.

Green Infrastructure

13

Interconnected networks of green roofs, bioswales, and urban forests providing natural stormwater and cooling services.

Complete Streets

14

Streets designed to enable safe, attractive, and comfortable access for users of all ages, abilities, and modes.

District Cooling System

15

Centralized generation and distribution of chilled water through underground insulated pipes to cool neighborhood buildings.

Autonomous Shuttle

16

Self-driving electric multi-passenger pods operating on pre-mapped routes to connect transit hubs with neighborhoods.

Active Travel Network

17

Protected, segregated cycling tracks and pedestrian greenways encouraging human-powered daily commuting.

Decarbonized Freight

18

Urban distribution networks powered entirely by electric vehicles, hydrogen fuel cells, and zero-emission logistics.
