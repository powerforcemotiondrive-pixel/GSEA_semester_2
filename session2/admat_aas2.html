Grammar Point: Spatial Prepositions & Passive Voice for Pre-Flight Defect Reporting

Review core aerodynamic language structures and complete the diagnostic Part 5 exam question.

Aerospace Language Clinic B1–B2 Grammar Focus
Aircraft Inspection Formula
Subject + is/are/was/were + Past Participle (+ Prepositional Location)

Key Communicative Guidelines:

  • 1. Spatial Prepositions for Airframe Anatomy: Locates external parts precisely ('Leading-edge slats are mounted along the forward wing skin; trailing-edge flaps are situated adjacent to the engine pylon').
  • 2. Present Passive for Structural Functions: Explains standard operational mechanisms ('Hydraulic actuators are powered by redundant engine-driven pumps').
  • 3. Past Passive for Walkaround Defect Reports: Documents pre-departure technical anomalies ('A hydraulic leak was observed near the main gear brake lines').
  • 4. Modal Passive for Maintenance Clearance: Mandates safety release actions ('All inspection panels must be securely fastened before the aircraft is cleared for pushback').
⚠️ TOEIC Part 5 Passive Voice Alert: Be sure to check subject-verb agreement with collective or plural aviation nouns (e.g., 'The landing gear IS inspected' vs. 'The main gear assemblies ARE inspected').
Authentic Operational Examples
