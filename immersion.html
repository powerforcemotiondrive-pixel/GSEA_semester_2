English Immersion & Student Destination Handbook

At the conclusion of Semester 2, all GSEA students participate in an intensive 4-week English immersion program across accredited academies in the United Kingdom and Ireland. Designed as a transformative linguistic accelerator, this program builds daily conversational fluency and provides dedicated preparation for the TOEIC examination at the beginning of Semester 3.

Travel Preparation & Homestay Guidelines

1. Travel Documentation

Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months. For the UK, check the latest ETA requirements; for Ireland, EU/French identity rules apply.

2. The 100% English Commitment

Commit to speaking only English during your 4 weeks. Use downtime to note new idiomatic phrases and vocabulary in your learning portfolio.

3. Respecting Family Routines

Engage actively with your host family, communicate your schedule clearly, and participate in family conversations at dinner every evening.
