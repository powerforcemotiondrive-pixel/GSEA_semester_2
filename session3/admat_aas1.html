Grammar Point: Cause, Effect & Relative Clauses in Ground Operations

Construct clear spoken and written sentences explaining turnaround delays and defining GSE vehicles using formal linkers for professional operational reporting.

The Cause & Effect Framework B2 Professional Focus
Grammar Patterns
Due to / Owing to + [Noun Phrase] | [Cause] + led to / resulted in + [Noun]
  • 1. Formal Prepositional Causes (Due to / Owing to): Followed strictly by a noun phrase or gerund, never by a full subject+verb clause ("Due to heavy ramp congestion, the refueler was delayed").
  • 2. Result Verbs (Led to / Resulted in): Express the direct outcome of an event ("An overheated conveyor resulted in a 15-minute baggage delay").
  • 3. Defining Relative Clauses (Which / That): Used to clearly define airside vehicles without jargon ("The GPU is a mobile generator that supplies electricity to the cabin").
  • 4. Transitional Connectors (Consequently / As a result): Placed at the start of a sentence followed by a comma to introduce a consequence.
⚠️ Common Grammar Trap: Do not write due to because or follow due to with a full sentence! Say "Due to the storm" (noun phrase), NOT "Due to the storm was bad". For full clauses, use "Because the storm was severe...".
Ground Handling Examples
✓ "Owing to a mechanical defect on the pushback tractor, stand clearance was postponed by ten minutes."
✓ "The belt loader experienced an electrical failure, which prevented handlers from unloading the forward hold on schedule."
TOEIC Diagnostic Practice Part 5: Incomplete Sentences
Sample Item • Logistics & Operations Timed Pace: ~30s

Due to a temporary failure of the mobile ground power unit, cabin air conditioning ________ until the auxiliary system was activated.

(A) was suspended
(B) to suspend
(C) suspending
(D) suspends
Click to Reveal Correct Answer & Explanations
CORRECT ANSWER (A) was suspended

Grammar Analysis: The sentence requires a past passive finite verb: the air conditioning was suspended by ground handling crew until auxiliary power was restored.

Why the Other Choices Are Incorrect:
(B) to suspend: An infinitive cannot serve as the main finite verb of the clause.
(C) suspending: A present participle without an auxiliary verb cannot complete the predicate.
(D) suspends: Active present tense contradicts the past time anchor ("was activated") and the passive role of air conditioning.
TOEIC Strategy: Identify the passive receiver of the action ("air conditioning") and the past time marker ("was activated"), which necessitates was + past participle.
