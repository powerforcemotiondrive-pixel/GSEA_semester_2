GSEA Semester 1 Lesson Outline

Session 3 Lesson Plan (2 Hours)

A 2-hour session exploring visitor limits for popular destinations, city regulations against overcrowding, grammar for contrast and rules, TOEIC practice, and a group debate on sustainable island tourism.

0-10
1.

Part 1: Tourism Policy News & Session 2 Review (10 mins)

Discussing current headlines on municipal tourist limits and checking memory of Session 2 words (modal shift, decarbonization, externalities, lifecycle assessment).

10-35
2.

Part 2: Destination Pressure & Visitor Capacity (25 mins)

Analyzing carrying capacity data in Venice, Dubrovnik, Amsterdam, and Maya Bay to examine how visitor volume impacts local residents and natural sites.

35-60
3.

Part 3: Pitching New Tourism Policies to the Mayor (25 mins)

Teams pitch municipal policies (Airbnb quotas, peak-season daily entry levies, cruise ship bans) to the city council and defend against business objections.

60-80
4.

Part 4: Grammar Clinic — Causatives & Contrast Words (20 mins)

Structuring municipal regulations with causatives (make, have, require to) and balancing opposing interests with contrast prepositions (despite, in spite of, whereas).

80-110
5.

Part 5: DDRS Simulation — The Isla Serena Water Crisis (30 mins)

4-role round-table negotiation resolving an acute summer water shortage (320% surge) vs. airport runway expansion. Teams agree on a 3-point policy resolution.

110-120
6.

Part 6: 10-Minute Vocabulary Wrap-Up & Taboo Fluency Challenge (10 mins)

Rapid recall of the 18 overtourism terms with audio pronunciation and 45-second Taboo fluency game without saying forbidden words.
