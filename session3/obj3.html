Session 3 - Debate and Critical Thinking Objectives

By the end of this session, you will be able to engage in debates and critical discussions with confidence, use key vocabulary and grammar, and build the skills needed for your TOEIC test and Session 5 evaluation.

1

Practice Identifying Argument Structures & Cause/Effect in TOEIC Reading

Analyze editorial articles and opinion pieces to separate central claims from supporting evidence and assumptions.

2

Master Polite Debate Language, Concessions, and Rhetorical Questions

Use diplomatic phrasing to question assumptions, acknowledge opposing arguments, and clarify misunderstandings.

3

Use Conjunctions (although, because) vs. Prepositions (despite, due to)

Correctly choose between conjunctions requiring a full clause and prepositions followed by a noun phrase or gerund.

4

Debate Technological Innovation vs. Environmental Caution (DDRS)

Explore the balance between rapid tech adoption and the precautionary principle for public safety and ecology.

5

Participate in an Interactive Class Debate with Constructive Feedback

Deliver concise, persuasive spoken contributions during a team debate while staying polite and focused on the topic.
