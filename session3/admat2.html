Grammar Point: Gerunds and Infinitives & TOEIC Practice

Learn when to use an -ing verb (gerund) and when to use to + verb (infinitive) after common business verbs.

Verbs with -ing vs. to + Verb B1–B2 Grammar Focus
Two Different Verb Patterns
Verb + -ing | Verb + to + Verb | Preposition + -ing

Core Rules: In English, the first verb decides the form of the second verb:

  • 1. Verbs Followed by -ing: recommend, suggest, consider, avoid, postpone, delay (*recommend implementing*).
  • 2. Verbs Followed by to + Verb: decide, agree, aim, plan, refuse, promise, hesitate (*agree to invest*).
  • 3. Prepositions Always Take -ing: Any verb after a preposition (in, by, for, committed to, look forward to) must end in -ing.
⚠️ TOEIC Trap Alert: In French, people say "recommander de faire" with an infinitive. But in English, you cannot say "recommend to do". You must say recommend doing (-ing)!
Climate Policy & Debate Examples
✓ "European transport ministers suggested capping carbon subsidies for short-haul flights."
✓ "The airline coalition agreed to invest in hydrogen fuel cell prototypes."
TOEIC Diagnostic Practice Part 5: Incomplete Sentences
Sample Item • Reading Section Timed Pace: ~30s

Environmental delegates at the summit strongly recommended ________ strict carbon emission quotas on regional cargo airlines.

(A) implement
(B) implementing
(C) to implement
(D) implemented
💡 Click to Reveal Answer & TOEIC Strategy Analysis ▼
Correct Answer: (B) implementing

Why (B) is correct: The verb recommend must be followed by an -ing verb when another action comes right after it (recommended implementing).

Why the Other Choices Are Incorrect:
  • (A) implement: Base form; cannot come directly after recommended.
  • (C) to implement: While it sounds like French "recommander de", "recommend to do" is incorrect in English.
  • (D) implemented: Past form; cannot be the object of recommended.
📌 Quick Test Tip: Memorize the top TOEIC verbs that need -ing: recommend, suggest, consider, avoid, postpone, and appreciate.
