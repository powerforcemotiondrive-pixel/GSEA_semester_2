Session 3: Overtourism

Review the 18 key terms for Overtourism to build your fluency in discussions, professional reading, and TOEIC practice.

Carrying Capacity

01

The maximum number of visitors an area or historic city can welcome without damaging nature, local culture, or public infrastructure.

Overtourism

02

Excessive tourist numbers resulting in environmental harm, resident displacement, and diminished visitor experiences.

Visitor Dispersal

03

Encouraging tourists to visit less crowded areas or travel during off-peak seasons to spread economic benefits and avoid crowding.

Tourist Tax

04

A municipal levy assessed on overnight lodging or day visitors to fund public infrastructure and sanitation.

De-Marketing

05

Campaigns that discourage too many tourists or target respectful travelers instead of rowdy party crowds (e.g. Amsterdam's "Stay Away" campaign).

Resident Displacement

06

The pricing out of long-term local residents caused by short-term vacation rental conversions.

Heritage Conservation

07

The scientific and policy preservation of historical monuments and archaeological sites against tourist wear.

Advance Reservation

08

A mandatory timed-entry booking system used by museums and national parks to control hourly crowd density.

Cruise Ship Ceiling

09

A statutory limit placed on the number of large cruise vessels allowed to berth at a port daily.

Seasonal Compression

10

The high concentration of visitor traffic into a brief summer or holiday window, straining local resources.

Spatial Saturation

11

Severe physical overcrowding where visitor numbers overwhelm streets, historic sites, and public amenities.

Community Stewardship

12

Local resident participation in formulating municipal tourism guidelines and revenue allocations.

Dynamic Pricing

13

Fluctuating ticket tariffs calibrated to demand to incentivize off-peak visiting hours.

Infrastructure Strain

14

The overwhelming of sewage, potable water, waste collection, and electrical grids during high season.

Short-Term Vacation Rental (STVR)

15

Furnished residential apartments rented to tourists on a nightly basis through digital booking platforms.

Authenticity Erosion

16

The transformation of genuine residential neighborhoods into commercialized souvenir corridors.

Zoning Ordinance

17

Municipal legal statutes establishing where commercial hospitality establishments may legally operate.

Aquifer Depletion

18

The severe loss of underground freshwater reserves caused by summer tourism demand and resort overuse.
