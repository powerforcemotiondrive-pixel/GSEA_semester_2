Official Assessment Rubric Total: 20 Points • B2 CEFR Standard

How Your 20 Points Are Awarded

Grading is transparent and criteria-referenced against official CEFR B2 descriptors. Your score out of 20 reflects your oral performance during the 12-minute technical pitch, your ability to handle spontaneous examiner Q&A, active use of required Module 3 terminology, and verifiable evidence of ongoing portfolio maintenance and TOEIC preparation.

Detailed Assessment Grid (6 Core Criteria)

Total: 20 Points
Criterion Points What the Teacher Evaluates (B2 Standard)
1. Fluency & Spoken Delivery
Flow, transitions & pacing 		4.0 pts Speech flows naturally without awkward hesitations or long pauses. Seamless transitions between system architecture, quantitative benefits, and DDRS dimensions. Strictly no reading aloud from slides or written note sheets!
2. Pronunciation & Phonology
Intelligibility & word stress 		3.0 pts Clear articulation and correct syllable stress on specialized technical terms (al-go-RI-thm, au-TO-no-mous, bi-o-ME-tric, CRY-o-ge-nic, scal-a-BI-li-ty, pro-PUL-sion). Intelligible English rhythm with natural sentence phrasing.
3. Grammatical Precision & Syntactic Range
Passives, future forms & clauses 		4.0 pts AG: Accurate technical Passive Voice (data is ingested, algorithms are fine-tuned), Future Forms with Predictive Certainty (is bound to, is projected to, will inevitably), Cautious Hedging & Concession markers (admittedly... nonetheless), and Defining/Non-defining Relative Clauses.
AAS: Precise modal verbs of obligation, permission, and prohibition and accurate ATM procedural phraseology.
4. Thematic & Technical Vocabulary
Target session vocabulary 		4.0 pts Active, natural deployment of at least 6 to 8 specialized technical terms from Module 3 (e.g. predictive maintenance, digital twin, cryogenic storage, distributed propulsion, vertiport, algorithmic bias, explainable AI, biometric verification, decentralized identity). Avoidance of repetitive generic words.
5. Interaction, Teamwork & Q&A
50/50 balance & defense 		2.5 pts Strict 50/50 speaking balance (~6 min each). Natural handovers and mutual support between partners. Spontaneous, articulate defense during examiner Q&A without switching to French or leaving one partner to answer alone.
6. Learning Portfolio & Self-Critique
Logs & self-evaluation 		2.5 pts Complete, organized Vocabulary Log with recorded Module 3 definitions. Verifiable evidence of ongoing TOEIC practice drills and an honest, constructive self-evaluation of personal language progress with 2 actionable goals.
Total Evaluation Mark 20 / 20 Contributes directly toward your official semester Formative Evaluation grade.

Performance Bands & Grade Descriptors

EXCELLENT (B2+) 18–20 pts

Outstanding technical clarity, spontaneous delivery without notes, advanced lexis seamlessly embedded, precise grammar, confident Q&A defense, exemplary 50/50 teamwork, and complete portfolio evidence.

Exceeds B2 benchmark
SOLID (B2 Standard) 15–17.5 pts

Clear and coherent technical presentation, good lexical range with 6+ terms, minor grammar slips that do not impede comprehension, capable handling of Q&A, balanced speaking time, and solid portfolio habits.

Meets official B2 standard
APPROACHING B2 / B1+ 12–14.5 pts

Understandable presentation but occasionally over-reliant on slide text or notes; basic vocabulary deployed with limited range; noticeable pauses during Q&A; uneven speaking balance; portfolio partially completed.

Requires consolidation
BELOW B2 < 12 pts

Frequent reading aloud from written scripts; frequent breakdowns in communication; lack of required technical lexis; inability to respond to teacher questions in English; incomplete or missing portfolio.

Action plan required
