Official Assessment Rubric Total: 20 Points • B2 CEFR Standard

How Your 20 Points Are Awarded

Grading is transparent and criteria-referenced. Your score out of 20 reflects your oral performance during the 12-minute presentation, your ability to handle spontaneous teacher Q&A, active use of required vocabulary, and verifiable evidence of ongoing portfolio maintenance and TOEIC preparation.

Detailed Assessment Grid (6 Core Criteria)

Total: 20 Points
Criterion Points What the Teacher Evaluates (B2 Standard)
1. Fluency & Coherence
Flow, pacing & transitions 		4 pts Speech flows naturally without long unnatural pauses or excessive hesitation. Ideas are connected smoothly using appropriate discourse markers (on the other hand, consequently, furthermore, to sum up). Delivery is communicative and spontaneous, without relying on reading written text.
2. Pronunciation & Articulation
Intelligibility & word stress 		3 pts Speech is clearly intelligible and easily understood by the listener. Correct syllable stress on technical terms (e.g., infrastrUcture, environmEntal, itInerary, catAstrophe). Sentence intonation sounds natural with clear phrasing.
3. Grammar & Sentence Structure
Accuracy & complex forms 		4 pts AG: Accurate Word Forms (-tion, -ment, -able, -ly), subject-verb agreement with collective nouns (the council decides), gerunds vs. infinitives (advocate retrofitting, decide to fund), and conditional hypotheticals.
AAS: Precise aerodynamic cause-and-effect connectors, comparative fleet data modifiers, and accident sequence narrative tenses.
4. Vocabulary & Topic Lexis
Target session vocabulary 		4 pts Active, natural deployment of at least 6 to 8 specialized technical terms from Module 2 (e.g., heatwave, flash flood, storm surge, permeable pavement, sponge city, 15-minute city, district cooling, biodiversity net gain, bioswale, wildlife hazard management). Avoidance of repetitive generic words.
5. Interaction & Teamwork
50/50 balance & teacher Q&A 		2.5 pts Seamless collaboration between partners with equal speaking time (~6 mins each). Natural handovers and active listening. Spontaneous, thoughtful responses during the teacher Q&A without switching to French or looking at the other partner to answer for them.
6. Portfolio & TOEIC Evidence
Logs & self-evaluation 		2.5 pts Student brings clear, up-to-date evidence of their Learning Portfolio (recorded vocabulary definitions, session notes) and documented TOEIC practice drills. Arrives ready with a mature self-critique of personal strong points and areas to improve.
Total Evaluation Mark 20 / 20 Contributes directly toward your official semester Formative Evaluation grade.

Performance Bands & Grade Descriptors

Exemplary 18–20 pts

Confident, spontaneous delivery. Smooth transitions and clear signposting. Broad vocabulary with seamless course terms. High grammatical accuracy. Balanced 50/50 teamwork. Portfolio fully updated with regular TOEIC practice.

Upper B2 / Approaching C1
Solid B2 Standard 14–17 pts

Clear, organized presentation with minor hesitations. Good use of topic vocabulary with minor slips. Complex grammar attempted with good control. Good partner balance. Portfolio maintained with basic self-evaluation ready.

Confident B2 Working Standard
Developing 10–13 pts

Noticeable pauses or reliance on reading notes. Limited range of topic vocabulary. Grammatical errors occasionally obscure meaning. Speaking time somewhat uneven. Incomplete portfolio or vague self-critique.

Borderline B1–B2 Range
Needs Improvement < 10 pts

Reads entire presentation from slides/paper. Severe grammar or pronunciation barriers. Presentation significantly under or over time. One partner dominates completely. No portfolio or TOEIC evidence provided.

Examiner Insider Tips: How to Maximize Your 20 Points

Make Eye Contact

Examiners immediately recognize when students read from hidden notes. Speak directly to the teacher—even if you make a minor grammar slip, your fluency score will remain high.

Pronounce Confidently

Click the audio speech buttons on this site's vocabulary pages before the tutorial so you are 100% confident with stress and pronunciation on technical words.

Support Your Partner

If your partner forgets a point, nod supportively or politely say: "And in addition to what [Name] said..." examiners award high interaction marks for good teamwork.

Be Honest in Self-Critique

Saying "I still struggle with past participle vs. past simple in timed reading" shows self-awareness. Teachers respect students who know their exact targets.
