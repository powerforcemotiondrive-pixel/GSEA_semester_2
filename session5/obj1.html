Target Competencies • B2 CEFR Level Formative Assessment Goals

What You Are Expected to Demonstrate

The Formative Evaluation measures both your collaborative spoken English (delivering a well-structured presentation and handling spontaneous Q&A) and your autonomous learning habits (actively recording target vocabulary, completing regular TOEIC drills, and critically evaluating your personal language development).

5 Core Learning Objectives (B2 Standards)

01

Deliver a Structured, Collaborative Oral Presentation

Spoken Production

Present your itinerary or comparative city analysis clearly over 10–12 minutes without reading written paragraphs. Use visual aids effectively, connect your arguments with logical discourse markers (on the one hand, consequently, in addition, to summarize), and maintain a balanced 50/50 division of speaking time with your partner.

02

Engage in Spontaneous Interactive Dialogue (Teacher Q&A)

Spoken Interaction

Respond spontaneously and accurately to follow-up questions from the teacher. Explain the reasoning behind your decisions, evaluate trade-offs, and comfortably build on or clarify your partner's points without switching languages.

03

Activate Specialized Vocabulary from the Platform Lists

Lexical Range

Naturally integrate at least 4 to 6 specific vocabulary terms from this course into your speech. Use accurate collocations, pronounce technical terms correctly, and explain complex concepts with precision rather than vague words like "good" or "things".

04

Demonstrate Grammatical Accuracy & Range (Track-Specific)

Grammatical Control

General English (AG): Show confident use of complex structures such as conditionals (if/would), passive voice ("sustainable guidelines were introduced..."), and comparative clauses.
Technical English (AT): Show high accuracy with modal verbs of obligation, permission, and prohibition (must, are required to, are prohibited from) and precise prepositions of movement and place.

05

Exhibit Autonomous Portfolio Habits & Constructive Self-Critique

Autonomous Learning

Present an organized portfolio with recorded session terms, personal reflections, and evidence of regular TOEIC practice. Arrive ready to discuss your personal strong points and weak points honestly, demonstrating that you understand where you need to focus next.

🎯 Linguistic Focus Comparison by Program

General English Track (AG)
  • Grammar Complexity: Deploy conditionals, relative pronouns, passive sentences, and comparative contrast structures.
  • Topic Lexis: Environmental certifications, carbon auditing, overtourism management, seasonal dispersal, and sustainable mobility.
  • Discussion Fluency: Expressing degrees of agreement, qualifying opinions, and summarizing complex social data.
Technical English Track (AAS)
  • Aviation Lexis: Active use of aerodrome terms (airside/landside, apron, manoeuvring area, taxiway centerline, runway designator, pushback, FOD).
  • Regulatory Precision: Deploying modal verbs of obligation, permission, and prohibition with zero ambiguity.
  • Spatial Prepositions: Accurate deployment of spatial prepositions (at the gate, on the apron, across the runway, along the taxiway).
💡

Tips on How to Meet Every Objective During Your 30 Minutes

Structure Your Talk Clearly

Start with a clear 30-second roadmap: "In today's presentation, we will first introduce the transport options, then analyze eco-accommodations, and finally evaluate the DDRS carbon impact."

Highlight Course Words

Don't be shy about emphasizing key terms from this site. When you use words like greenwashing, resident backlash, or runway incursion, say them clearly and proudly.

Have Your Portfolio Open

Show you take responsibility for your learning. Point to your vocabulary log and your recent TOEIC scores so the teacher can see your steady semester progress.
