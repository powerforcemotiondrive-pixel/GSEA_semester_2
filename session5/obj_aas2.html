Target Competencies • B2 CEFR Level Technical English Evaluation Goals

What You Are Expected to Demonstrate (AAS FE 2)

The Formative Evaluation measures both your collaborative technical communication (delivering a well-structured technical aircraft or airline analysis and answering spontaneous operational questions) and your autonomous learning habits (actively recording course vocabulary, participating in class, and critically evaluating your personal language development).

5 Core Learning Objectives (B2 Standards)

01

Deliver a Structured Technical Aircraft or Airline Presentation

Spoken Production

Co-present a logical 12–15 minute briefing with balanced speaking time, clear discourse signposting, and crisp visual slides without reading verbatim text.

02

Explain Aerodynamic Forces, Control Surfaces & Systems

Technical Fluency

Describe wing lift generation, primary and secondary control surfaces (ailerons, elevators, rudder, flaps, slats, spoilers), and redundant systems with spatial and functional clarity.

03

Accurately Deploy B2 Grammar for Comparison, Cause & Chronology

Grammar Range

Use comparative and superlative structures for fleet evaluation, cause-and-effect connectors for aerodynamic phenomena, and narrative past tenses for incident chronologies.

04

Deploy Specialized Aeronautical Lexis Naturally

Technical Lexis

Active, accurate integration of at least 6–8 specialized terms from Module 2 (aerodynamics, aircraft anatomy, commercial fleets, accident investigation) during the talk and Q&A.

05

Demonstrate Spontaneous Interaction & Maintain Vocabulary Portfolio

Interaction & Autonomous Study

Handle teacher follow-up questions flexibly without scripted answers, support your partner collaboratively, and present a thorough personal vocabulary notebook.
