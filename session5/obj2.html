Target Competencies • B2 CEFR Level Formative Assessment Goals

What You Are Expected to Demonstrate

The Formative Evaluation measures both your collaborative spoken English (delivering a well-structured presentation and handling spontaneous Q&A) and your autonomous learning habits (actively recording target vocabulary, completing regular TOEIC drills, and critically evaluating your personal language development).

5 Core Learning Objectives (B2 Standards)

01

Deliver a Collaborative Climate Resilience Masterplan

Spoken Production

Present your prepared Climate Resilience Masterplan (Option A: Airport or Option B: City) clearly over 10–12 minutes without reading verbatim notes or slides. Systematically address the 4 core dimensions (Hazard Profile, Physical/Civil Engineering, Nature-Based Solutions, and Contingency Operations), connect your proposals with logical discourse markers (consequently, in the event of, to mitigate against, as a secondary safeguard), and maintain a balanced 50/50 division of speaking time with your partner.

02

Engage in Spontaneous Technical Defense (Teacher Q&A)

Spoken Interaction

Respond spontaneously and accurately to follow-up questions from the teacher regarding project viability, budget trade-offs, and ecological trade-offs. Defend engineering priorities, employ hedging and concession strategies ("Admittedly, while initial capital expenditure is substantial, the lifecycle ROI and flood damage mitigation justify the investment..."), and comfortably build on or clarify your partner's points without hesitation.

03

Activate Specialized Climate & Resilience Vocabulary

Lexical Range

Naturally integrate at least 6 to 8 specialized vocabulary terms from Module 2 into your presentation and Q&A (e.g., sponge city infrastructure, bioswales, storm surge barrier, heat island mitigation, microgrid redundancy, runway FOD sweep, permeable pavements, flash flood retention). Pronounce terms accurately and deploy technical collocations with B2-level precision rather than imprecise colloquialisms.

04

Demonstrate Grammatical Precision & Syntactic Range (B2 Core)

Grammatical Control

Deploy complex syntactic structures with high accuracy: 1) Morphological word forms (mitigate/mitigation, resilient/resilience, permeable/permeability), 2) Collective noun agreement ("the airport authority has commissioned" / "the municipal council are divided"), 3) Accurate gerunds vs infinitives (prioritize retrofitting, aim to reinforce), and 4) Conditionals for risk modeling ("If storm intensity were to exceed baseline projections, subterranean holding tanks would divert runoff...").

05

Exhibit Autonomous Portfolio Habits & Constructive Self-Critique

Autonomous Learning

Present an organized physical or digital portfolio containing your recorded Module 2 vocabulary logs, session reflection notes, and verified TOEIC practice records. Articulate an honest self-evaluation of your spoken English strengths, identify specific recurring error patterns, and outline concrete action targets for Semester 2.

🎯 Focus Areas by Option (Airport vs City)

Option A: Airport Climate Resilience
  • Hazard & Asset Vulnerability: Runway inundation, extreme heat tarmac rutting, crosswinds, apron flash flooding, bird strikes during storm displacement.
  • Engineering & Redundancy: Rapid-drain porous asphalt, seawalls, independent solar microgrids, dual-circuit de-icing fluid retention basins.
  • Operational Continuity: A-CDM irregular operations protocols, divert capacity management, emergency passenger welfare safeguarding.
Option B: Urban Climate Resilience
  • Urban Heat & Water Management: Urban heat island effect, bioswale streetscapes, permeable pavements, subterranean stormwater holding tanks.
  • Nature-Based Solutions: Urban tree canopy expansion, green roofs, daylighting buried streams, urban biodiversity corridors.
  • Citizen Safeguards & Transit: Multimodal evacuation corridors, cool-roof public shelters, smart grid flood alerts for low-lying metro stations.
💡

Tips on How to Meet Every Objective During Your 30 Minutes

Structure Your Talk Clearly

Start with a clear 30-second roadmap: "In today's evaluation, we will first assess Nice Airport's coastal flood hazards, examine sea wall and stormwater holding tank engineering, analyze nature-based bioswales, and conclude with contingency flight operations."

Highlight Course Words

Don't hesitate to clearly emphasize Module 2 technical lexis: sponge city infrastructure, bioswale, thermal stress, storm surge barrier, runoff retention, microgrid redundancy.

Have Your Portfolio Open

Demonstrate autonomous academic habits: present your Module 2 vocabulary log, session reflection notes, and TOEIC error tracker with tangible self-study hours.
