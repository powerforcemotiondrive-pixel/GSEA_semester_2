Formative Evaluation 2 (FE 2) AT Module 2 • Continuous Assessment

Aircraft / Airlines In-Depth Profile Presentation

In pairs or trios, you will deliver a structured 12 to 15-minute spoken presentation analyzing either a specific commercial or transport aircraft (aerodynamics, airframe anatomy, flight control innovations, systems) or a major airline (fleet composition, manufacturer orders, operating model, route network). You will then defend your analysis during a 3 to 5-minute teacher Q&A session.

30-Minute Slot Architecture

01 • 12–15 Minutes

Joint Spoken Delivery

Both partners must speak equally (~6–7 mins each). Strictly no reading from pre-written scripts or full slides.

02 • 3–5 Minutes

Teacher Operational Q&A

Spontaneous answers demonstrating technical comprehension, systems knowledge, and B2 conversational fluency.

03 • 5–8 Minutes

Feedback & Self-Critique

Immediate formative debrief, vocabulary log review, and personal assessment against the Continuous Assessment criteria.

Required Presentation Components (Choose Option A or Option B)

Option A: Specific Aircraft Type

e.g. A350, 787, A220, ATR 72
  • Airframe & Aerodynamics: Wing design (camber, winglets/sharklets), high-lift devices (slats/flaps), composite materials.
  • Key Figures & Specifications: MTOW, passenger capacity, range, engine bypass ratio, cruise Mach speed.
  • Systems & Flight Deck: Avionics architecture, fly-by-wire flight control laws, hydraulic redundancy.
  • Historical Milestones & Safety: Maiden flight, entry into service, commercial success, notable incidents and lessons learned.

Option B: Major Commercial Airline

e.g. Air France, Emirates, Ryanair, Delta
  • Fleet Composition: Narrowbody vs. widebody breakdown, single-type vs. mixed fleets, average aircraft age.
  • Business Model: Legacy network carrier (hub-and-spoke) vs. ultra-low-cost carrier (point-to-point), ancillary revenue strategy.
  • Manufacturer Strategy: Airbus vs. Boeing orders, new generation transitions (e.g., A350 replacing 777-200ER).
  • Operational History & Safety Record: Founding milestones, alliance memberships (SkyTeam, Star Alliance, oneworld), safety record analysis.

Mandatory Deliverables

1. Presentation Slide Deck

Clean visual slides (diagrams, fleet charts, route maps). Maximum 6–8 slides. Highly visual with bullet key figures — strictly no blocks of running text. Submit to your teacher 24 hours prior.

2. Personal Vocabulary Log

Bring your updated personal vocabulary book containing at least 25 newly mastered technical terms from Module 2 (aerodynamics, aircraft parts, fleet economics, and safety). Assessed as Criterion 6.
