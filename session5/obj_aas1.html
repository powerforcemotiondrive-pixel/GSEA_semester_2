Target Competencies • B2 CEFR Level Technical English Evaluation Goals

What You Are Expected to Demonstrate (AAS FE 1)

The Formative Evaluation measures both your collaborative technical communication (delivering a well-structured technical airport analysis and answering spontaneous operational questions) and your autonomous learning habits (actively recording course vocabulary, participating in class, and critically evaluating your personal language development).

5 Core Learning Objectives (B2 Standards)

01

Deliver a Structured Technical Airport Profile Presentation

Spoken Production

Present your chosen airport's infrastructure, runways, taxiway networks, and apron operations clearly over 10–12 minutes without reading written notes. Effectively utilize official aerodrome charts and visual diagrams, link arguments with logical discourse markers (consequently, in addition, furthermore, regarding runway configuration), and maintain a balanced 50/50 division of speaking time with your partner.

02

Engage in Spontaneous Operational Dialogue (Teacher Q&A)

Spoken Interaction

Respond spontaneously and accurately to follow-up questions from the teacher regarding airport layout trade-offs, published runway hot spots, ATC visibility constraints, and surface incident root causes without switching to French.

03

Activate Module 1 Aerodrome Vocabulary (Sessions 1 to 4)

Lexical Range

Naturally integrate at least 6 to 8 specialized technical terms from Sessions 1–4 (e.g. runway designators, holding points, stop bars, turnaround time, GPU, pushback tug, FOD, ATCO separation authority, AFISO advisory service). Pronounce terms with correct syllable stress and accurate technical meaning.

04

Demonstrate Grammatical Precision & Functional Structures

Grammar Control

Deploy accurate B2 functional grammar: modal verbs of obligation and necessity (must, should, are required to), past passive voice when analyzing historical incidents ("The runway was occupied when the clearance was issued"), cause-and-effect linkers (due to, led to, resulted in), and responsibility formulas (is responsible for + -ing, tasked with).

05

Exhibit Portfolio Habits & Autonomous Self-Critique

Autonomous Learning

Present an updated vocabulary log documenting technical terms learned across Module 1. Arrive prepared to discuss your personal linguistic strong points and 1–2 concrete weak points / pronunciation goals constructively with your teacher.

Notice on TOEIC Practice:

TOEIC testing is not evaluated or graded during this speaking tutorial. Criterion 6 focuses on your vocabulary study log, course preparation, and self-critique. Independent TOEIC practice remains accessible across the platform for ongoing exam readiness.
