Continuous Assessment Criteria (Aircraft / Airlines Task)

Assessment is criteria-referenced across 6 core technical communication and autonomous study competencies. Your total score out of 20 contributes directly to your official semester grade.

Criterion Pts Examiner Expectations (B2 CEFR • Technical English)
1. Fluency & Coherence 4 Presentation flows naturally without long unnatural pauses. Ideas linked logically with discourse markers (regarding the aerodynamic profile, consequently, in contrast, to conclude). Strictly no reading from notes or slides!
2. Pronunciation & Phonetic Clarity 3 Clear articulation, appropriate word stress on polysyllabic lexis (ae·ro·DY·na·mics, com·PO·site, al·TI·tude), intelligible delivery with confident projection.
3. Grammatical Range & Accuracy 4 Accurate comparative and superlative evaluations, past narrative tenses for incident chronologies, and cause/effect connectors.
4. Vocabulary & Topic Lexis 4 Active, accurate deployment of at least 6 to 8 specialized technical terms from Module 2 (aerodynamics, aircraft anatomy, commercial fleets, accident investigation).
5. Interaction & Teamwork 2.5 Equal 50/50 balance between partners (~6–7 mins each), smooth handovers, and spontaneous, thoughtful responses during teacher Q&A.
6. Portfolio & Self-Critique 2.5 Updated personal vocabulary log with Module 2 terms, and mature self-critique of strong and weak points. (Note: TOEIC testing does not factor into this grade).
Total Score 20 Contributes directly to your official Semester 1 Formative Evaluation grade.

Performance Standard Bands

Exemplary (18–20 pts)

Fluent, spontaneous delivery; precise technical aerospace lexis; high grammatical accuracy; 50/50 teamwork; portfolio fully updated.

Solid B2 (14–17 pts)

Clear presentation; good course vocabulary; good grammar control with minor slips; balanced teamwork; portfolio maintained.

Developing (10–13 pts)

Frequent pauses, limited lexis, recurring grammar slips, uneven partner balance, incomplete vocabulary portfolio.

Needs Imp. (<10 pts)

Reads presentation directly, severe communication barriers, one partner silent, missing vocabulary log.
