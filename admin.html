Dynamic Formative Evaluation (FE) Rotations:

To ensure variety in group work and timeslot fairness, groups do not have fixed pairs or trios. Group A rotates across 2 pairs + 2 trios (4 slots = 2h), and Group B rotates across 3 pairs + 1 trio (4 slots = 2h). Every student experiences different peers and rotates between early and late slots across FE1 and FE2. Switch to the FE Tutorial Rotas tab to view your complete evaluation schedule.

30-Minute Oral Tutorial Timetables

Every student group has an exact 30-minute appointment slot with their assigned teacher. To guarantee fairness, pairings and trios rotate across FE1 and FE2, alternating early and late slots. Group A consists of 2 pairs + 2 trios (10 students) and Group B consists of 3 pairs + 1 trio (9 students), precisely fitting 4 slots of 30 minutes (2 hours total).

Essential Checklist for Formative Evaluation Day

1. Arrive Early

Be outside the examination room 5 minutes before your scheduled start time.

2. Visual Supports

Have your slides or handouts ready to display immediately. Do not read from notes!

3. Vocabulary & TOEIC

Have your vocabulary logs and completed TOEIC practice records open and ready for inspection.

4. Self-Critique Ready

Arrive with your personal strong points and weak points clearly thought through to discuss.
