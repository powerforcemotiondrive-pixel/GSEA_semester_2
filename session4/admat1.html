Grammar Point: Prepositions of Place & TOEIC Practice

Learn how to choose between AT, IN, and ON for locations and facilities, and avoid common movement vs. position traps in TOEIC Part 5.

AT, IN, and ON for Places B1–B2 Grammar Focus
The Three Place Prepositions
AT (Point / Desk) • IN (Enclosed Room / City) • ON (Surface / Floor / Line)

Core Rules: Prepositions of place tell us where something is located. In travel, transit, and workplace contexts, follow these three rules:

  • 1. AT (Specific Points & Desks): Exact service counters, meeting points, and transit stops (at the desk, at the solar charging hub, at the gate).
  • 2. IN (Enclosed Spaces & Areas): Rooms, buildings, cities, and regions (in the terminal, in the national park, in Copenhagen).
  • 3. ON (Surfaces, Floors & Public Transport): Flat surfaces, specific floors, and public transit (on the roof, on the second floor, on the train).
  • 4. Relational Words: next to / adjacent to (beside), between (between two points), and throughout (all across an area).
⚠️ TOEIC Trap Alert: Watch out for movement words! Static places use at, in, or on. Movement uses to, into, onto, or toward. If the action is stationary (e.g., purchase, wait, meet, sit), do not choose movement words!
Green Tourism & Transit Examples
✓ "Visitors can collect reusable guidebooks at the eco-tourism kiosk located in the primary lobby."
✓ "Electric shuttle buses run continuously between the high-speed rail terminus and the regional biosphere reserve."
TOEIC Diagnostic Practice Part 5: Incomplete Sentences
Sample Item • Reading Section Timed Pace: ~30s

Visitors attending the Green Mobility Exhibition may purchase discounted electric shuttle passes ________ the information desk in the central arrivals concourse.

(A) at
(B) into
(C) onto
(D) toward
💡 Click to Reveal Answer & TOEIC Strategy Analysis ▼
Correct Answer: (A) at

Why (A) is correct: The verb "purchase" describes an action happening at a specific point or service counter ("the information desk"). In English, we use at for specific service desks, counters, and stations.

Why the Other Choices Are Incorrect:
  • (B) into: Means moving from the outside to the inside of an enclosed space (into a room).
  • (C) onto: Means moving to the top of a surface (onto a platform).
  • (D) toward: Shows movement in a direction, not where a stationary action takes place.
📌 Quick Test Tip: Check if the action involves moving or staying in one place. If the action is stationary (e.g., purchase, wait, meet), eliminate movement prepositions (into, onto, toward).
