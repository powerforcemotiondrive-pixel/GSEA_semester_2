Grammar Point: Collocations & Word Partnerships & TOEIC Practice

Learn how words naturally pair together in professional English, especially verbs that need a specific preposition (with, to, in).

Words That Go Together (Collocations) B1–B2 Grammar Focus
Key Word Pairs
Verb + Preposition: comply with / adhere to | Verb + Noun: meet standards / conduct an audit

Core Rules: Certain verbs and words always travel in pairs in business English:

  • 1. Verbs with Fixed Prepositions: comply with (rules), adhere to (policies), participate in (events), account for (a percentage).
  • 2. Verbs with NO Preposition: meet standards (NOT meet with standards), follow rules, observe regulations.
  • 3. Natural Business Pairs: conduct an audit, grant clearance, implement measures, strict confidentiality.
⚠️ TOEIC Trap Alert: Pay close attention to the preposition right after the blank! If you see with, you cannot pick meet, follow, or observe (they don't use with). You must pick comply with!
Biometrics & Security Examples
✓ "Border control e-gates must comply with international encryption protocols." (Verb + Prep)
✓ "The airport authority will conduct a comprehensive audit of its facial scanner feeds." (Verb + Noun)
TOEIC Diagnostic Practice Part 5: Incomplete Sentences
Sample Item • Reading Section Timed Pace: ~30s

To guarantee the security of decentralized passenger credentials, all biometric scanning hardware installed at border e-gates must ________ with international encryption standards.

(A) comply
(B) observe
(C) meet
(D) follow
💡 Click to Reveal Answer & TOEIC Strategy Analysis ▼
Correct Answer: (A) comply

Why (A) is correct: The blank is immediately followed by the preposition "with" (with international encryption standards). In professional English, the correct verb pair is comply with. The other verbs (observe, meet, follow) cannot be followed by "with".

Why the Other Choices Are Incorrect:
  • (B) observe: Direct verb; takes an object with NO preposition (e.g., observe standards).
  • (C) meet: Direct verb; we say meet standards, not meet with standards.
  • (D) follow: Direct verb; takes an object with NO preposition (e.g., follow standards).
📌 Quick Test Tip: When all 4 choices have similar meanings (rules/standards), look at the preposition after the blank: comply WITH, adhere TO, conform TO, but meet/follow [NO preposition].
