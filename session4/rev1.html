Session 4: Sustainable and Eco-tourism

Review the 18 key terms for Sustainable and Eco-tourism to build your fluency in discussions, professional reading, and TOEIC practice.

Eco-Certification

01

A third-party seal verifying that a tourism enterprise complies with rigorous environmental standards.

Greenwashing

02

Misleading marketing that portrays products or travel practices as environmentally friendly without empirical proof.

Carbon Offsetting

03

Compensating for carbon emissions through financial investment in certified re-forestation or clean energy projects.

Zero-Waste Travel

04

A travel methodology prioritizing reusable gear, local organic food, and composting to eliminate single-use waste.

Community-Based Tourism (CBT)

05

Tourism where local communities manage operations and receive direct economic proceeds.

Biodiversity Conservation

06

Protecting endangered fauna, flora, and native habitats from tourist disruption.

Low-Impact Hospitality

07

Accommodations designed with passive solar cooling, rainwater harvesting, and non-toxic materials.

Biodiversity Corridor

08

A protected natural strip connecting separated wildlife habitats to allow safe migration across tourist areas.

Slow Travel

09

A travel ethos emphasizing longer stays, surface transit, and deep cultural immersion over hurried flights.

Regenerative Tourism

10

Travel practices designed to actively improve and restore local ecosystems rather than merely minimizing harm.

Measurable Eco-Benefit (Additionality)

11

A real, measurable environmental improvement created by a conservation project that would not have happened on its own.

Carbon-Neutral Itinerary

12

A holiday schedule where all transit, food, and accommodation emissions have been eliminated or verified offset.

Native Habitat Restoration

13

Ecological work rehabilitating damaged terrain to re-establish indigenous flora and wildlife corridors.

Leave No Trace (LNT)

14

A set of seven universal outdoor ethics promoting minimal impact in wilderness exploration.

Fair Trade Tourism

15

Certification ensuring fair wages, safe labor conditions, and ethical treatment across tourism supply chains.

Renewable Powered Transit

16

Travel systems propelled exclusively by certified solar, wind, or hydro-electric generation.

Rewilding

17

Restoring natural wilderness ecosystems and re-introducing native species, often funded by low-impact tourism.

Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

18

The 17 United Nations global benchmarks guiding responsible tourism, poverty reduction, and climate action.
