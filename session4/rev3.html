Session 4: Biometrics and digital identity

Review the 18 key terms for Biometrics and digital identity to build your fluency in discussions, professional reading, and TOEIC practice.

Biometric Token

01

A digital credential containing encrypted mathematical representations of facial, iris, or fingerprint features.

Automated Border Control (e-Gate)

02

An automated security barrier verifying traveler passport chips against live biometric facial captures.

Seamless Travel Experience

03

A frictionless passenger journey through ticketing, bag drop, security, and boarding using biometric verification.

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

04

Strict European Union privacy law governing the collection, processing, and storage of personal biometric data.

Decentralized Identity (DID)

05

A self-sovereign digital identity architecture where travelers store their own credentials on personal devices.

Facial Recognition Matching

06

Comparing live digital video feeds against stored passport reference photos using deep neural networks.

Liveness Detection

07

Anti-spoofing software verifying that a biometric image belongs to a physically present living subject rather than a photo.

Data Sovereignty

08

The principle that digital information is subject to the laws and governance structures of the country where it is processed.

Passenger Consent Framework

09

An explicit, informed legal agreement where a passenger willingly opts into biometric screening with clear opt-out rights.

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

10

A security verification process requiring users to provide two separate forms of identification (e.g., password + biometric scan or SMS code) before granting account access.

False Match Rate (FMR)

11

The statistical percentage of biometric verification trials where the system incorrectly matches distinct individuals.

Identity Theft

12

The illegal acquisition, cloning, and fraudulent use of another individual's personal data, digital credentials, or biometric information for financial or criminal gain.

Demographic Disparity

13

Measurable performance discrepancies in facial recognition accuracy across different racial, gender, or age demographics.

Data Breach

14

A security incident in which sensitive, confidential, or biometric customer data is unlawfully accessed, extracted, copied, or transmitted by unauthorized malicious actors.

Surveillance Creep

15

The gradual expansion of a security technology beyond its original authorized scope into invasive public tracking.

Opt-Out Protocol

16

A mandatory alternative pathway allowing passengers to undergo traditional manual document checks without penalty.

Digital Travel Credential (DTC)

17

An official digital representation of a physical passport conforming to international civil aviation (ICAO) standards.

Digital Wallet

18

A secure mobile application that stores virtual identity credentials, digital passports, boarding passes, and payment cards on a traveler's personal smartphone.
