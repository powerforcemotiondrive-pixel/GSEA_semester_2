Grammar Point: Past Narrative Tenses & Time Linkers in Accident Chronologies

Review core aerodynamic language structures and complete the diagnostic Part 5 exam question.

Aerospace Language Clinic B1–B2 Grammar Focus
Accident Investigation Formula
While + Past Continuous, Past Simple || Subject + had + Past Participle (Past Perfect)

Key Communicative Guidelines:

  • 1. Past Continuous for Background Conditions: Sets the atmospheric and operational scene ('While the flight was cruising in severe icing conditions at FL350, the pitot probes iced over').
  • 2. Past Simple for Sequential Chain of Events: Recounts the chronological trigger events ('The autopilot disconnected, the master warning sounded, and the captain initiated recovery').
  • 3. Past Perfect for Pre-Existing Latent Conditions: Identifies maintenance or regulatory oversights prior to departure ('Investigators revealed that technicians had installed an incorrect adhesive during an overhaul six months earlier').
  • 4. Cause-and-Effect Narrative Subordinators: Connects human factors to systemic outcomes ('Because the flight crew had received insufficient simulator training on the new trim system, they failed to recognize the runaway trim').
⚠️ TOEIC Part 5 Narrative Tense Alert: When two actions happened in the past, the earlier event MUST use the Past Perfect ('had + verb3'), not the Past Simple ('The crew realized that the controller had assigned them the wrong heading').
Authentic Operational Examples
✓ While the aircraft was descending on approach, the ground proximity warning system alerted the crew to rising terrain.
✓ The BEA safety board determined that sensor discrepancy had triggered inappropriate flight control law degradation.
