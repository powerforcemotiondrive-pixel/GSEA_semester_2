Session 4: Significant Aviation Accidents & Aircraft Types

Master the 18 key vocabulary terms for Significant Aviation Accidents and Aircraft Types to analyze investigation methodologies, contributing factors, black box data, and safety lessons learned with technical precision and spoken fluency. Click any term to hear its pronunciation.

Accident

01

An occurrence associated with the operation of an aircraft causing serious injury, death, or major structural failure.

Incident

02

An occurrence, other than an accident, associated with aircraft operation that affects or could affect the safety of operation.

Flight Data Recorder (FDR)

03

A flight recorder device that records operating parameters (altitude, airspeed, heading, control positions) to reconstruct accidents.

Cockpit Voice Recorder (CVR)

04

A flight recorder device that records audio inside the cockpit, including pilot voices, radio communications, and alarms.

Investigation Bureau

05

An independent national safety authority (e.g. BEA in France, NTSB in the US) tasked with determining accident causes.

Root Cause

06

The fundamental underlying failure or condition that directly initiated the sequence resulting in the accident.

Contributing Factor

07

An environmental, mechanical, or human factor that increased the likelihood or severity of an accident.

Metal Fatigue

08

Progressive structural damage and cracking that occurs when metal airframe components are subjected to repeated cyclic stress.

Decompression

09

The loss of internal cabin pressure in an aircraft due to structural failure, door seal rupture, or system malfunction.

Engine Failure

10

The unexpected shutdown, flameout, or mechanical destruction of an aircraft engine during operation.

Bird Strike

11

A collision between an airborne animal (bird or bat) and an aircraft in flight or during takeoff/landing.

Icing

12

The accumulation of atmospheric ice on wings, control surfaces, or pitot probes, severely degrading aerodynamic performance.

Controlled Flight Into Terrain (CFIT)

13

An accident in which an airworthy aircraft under crew control is inadvertently flown into ground, water, or obstacles.

Loss of Control In-Flight (LOC-I)

14

A severe deviation from the intended flight path caused by aerodynamic stall, spatial disorientation, or systems failure.

Human Factors

15

The study of human capabilities, cognitive limitations, communication, and ergonomics in aviation operations.

Situational Awareness

16

An accurate mental perception of the aircraft position, flight condition, environmental threats, and systems status.

Safety Recommendation

17

A formal proposal by an accident investigation board recommending mandatory regulatory, operational, or design changes.

Airworthiness Directive (AD)

18

A legally enforceable regulation issued by a civil aviation authority mandating inspections or modifications to resolve an unsafe condition.
