Session 1: Introduction & Aviation Survey

Master the 18 key vocabulary terms for the Introduction & Aviation Survey to discuss security dilemmas, low-cost operations, alternative propulsion, and passenger rights with technical precision and spoken fluency. Click any term to hear its pronunciation.

Unruly Passenger

01

A disruptive traveler who refuses to follow crew instructions or threatens cabin safety.

Air Marshal

02

An undercover law enforcement officer deployed on flights to prevent hijackings and security threats.

Flight Deck Security

03

Physical barriers, reinforced cockpit doors, and strict entry rules that protect pilots from intruders.

Armed Pilot Protocol (FFDO)

04

A legal framework allowing qualified pilots to carry concealed firearms in the cockpit for defense.

Low-Cost Carrier (LCC)

05

An airline offering low base fares while charging extra for luggage, seat selection, and meals.

Ancillary Revenue

06

Extra money an airline makes from optional fees like baggage, snacks, and priority boarding.

Noise Abatement

07

Flight paths, climb angles, and night curfews designed to reduce engine noise over residential areas.

Decibel Footprint (EPNdB)

08

The measured noise area an aircraft produces during takeoff, flyover, and landing approach.

Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)

09

Clean jet fuel made from non-fossil waste or plants that significantly cuts net greenhouse gas emissions.

Hydrogen Propulsion

10

Aircraft engines that burn liquid hydrogen or use fuel cells to fly with zero in-flight carbon emissions.

Hybrid-Electric Aircraft

11

Aircraft combining fuel engines and battery motors to cut fuel use and noise during takeoff and climb.

Space Tourism

12

Commercial suborbital or orbital spaceflights carrying paying private passengers.

Suborbital Trajectory

13

A flight path that briefly reaches the edge of space before returning to Earth without entering orbit.

Passenger Rights (EU261)

14

Official rules requiring airlines to give cash compensation, meals, or hotels for long flight delays.

Denied Boarding (Bumping)

15

Refusing to let a ticketed passenger board a flight because the airline sold too many seats.

Slot Allocation

16

The scheduled permission granted to an airline by airport coordinators to land or take off at a congested hub.

Biometric Screening

17

Automated facial or fingerprint scanning at boarding gates to verify traveler identity quickly.

Turnaround Time (TAT)

18

The duration an aircraft remains parked at a gate between arriving from one flight and departing on the next.
