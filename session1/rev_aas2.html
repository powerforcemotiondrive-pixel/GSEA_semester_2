Session 1: Aerodynamics & Flight Principles

Master the 18 key vocabulary terms for Aerodynamics and Flight Principles to explain lift generation, the four fundamental forces, aircraft axes, and stall recovery procedures with technical precision and spoken fluency. Click any term to hear its pronunciation.

Lift

01

The aerodynamic upward force generated by the wings perpendicular to the relative airflow, overcoming weight.

Thrust

02

The forward force produced by the engines that overcomes aerodynamic drag.

Weight

03

The downward gravitational force pulling the aircraft toward the Earth, directly opposed by lift.

Drag

04

The retarding aerodynamic resistance force acting parallel and opposite to the direction of flight.

Angle of Attack (AoA)

05

The acute angle formed between the chord line of the airfoil and the oncoming relative wind.

Airfoil

06

A streamlined surface, such as an aircraft wing or rotor blade, designed to create lift as air flows over it.

Camber

07

The convex asymmetry or curvature between the upper and lower surfaces of an airfoil.

Boundary Layer

08

The thin layer of air immediately adjacent to the aircraft surface where viscous forces slow the airflow.

Stall

09

A sudden reduction in lift caused by exceeding the critical angle of attack, resulting in airflow separation from the upper wing.

Lateral Axis

10

The imaginary horizontal line running wingtip-to-wingtip around which the aircraft pitches up or down.

Longitudinal Axis

11

The imaginary line running from nose to tail around which the aircraft rolls or banks.

Vertical Axis

12

The imaginary vertical line running top-to-bottom through the center of gravity around which the aircraft yaws.

Pitch

13

The rotational movement around the lateral axis causing the nose of the aircraft to move up or down.

Roll

14

The rotational movement around the longitudinal axis causing the aircraft wings to bank left or right.

Yaw

15

The rotational movement around the vertical axis causing the aircraft nose to turn left or right.

Elevator

16

The movable horizontal flight control surface on the tail empennage used to control pitch.

Aileron

17

A hinged control surface on the trailing edge of each wing used to control roll and banking.

Rudder

18

The primary vertical flight control surface hinged to the fin used to control yaw.
