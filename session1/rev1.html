Session 1: Introduction & Travel Anecdotes

Review the 18 key terms for Introduction & Travel Anecdotes to build your fluency in discussions, professional reading, and TOEIC practice.

Itinerary

01

A planned route or journey, detailing scheduled stops, transport connections, and activities.

Layover

02

A period of waiting between two connecting flights or segments of a travel journey.

Catch the Travel Bug

03

To suddenly develop a strong enthusiasm, passion, or desire for traveling to new places.

Baggage Allowance

04

The maximum weight and volume of luggage a passenger can check in or carry on without penalty.

Off the Beaten Track

05

Traveling to secluded, quiet, or uncommon destinations away from crowded tourist hotspots.

Round-Trip

06

A journey to a destination and back to the original starting location.

Hit the Road

07

To depart on a journey, begin travel, or set out on a road trip.

Miss a Connection

08

To arrive too late for a scheduled connecting flight, train, or ferry due to prior delays.

All-Inclusive

09

A vacation package where meals, drinks, and resort leisure activities are bundled into one price.

Extend a Stay

10

To prolong the duration of a trip, holiday, or hotel booking beyond the original departure date.

In the Driver's Seat

11

To be in full control of a situation, project, or travel plan rather than letting events decide.

Customs Declaration

12

An official document listing goods being brought into a country for tax and security screening.

Boarding Pass

13

A document or digital barcode permitting a passenger to board an aircraft or vessel.

Direct Flight

14

A flight that travels between two cities without changing aircraft, though intermediate stops may occur.

Peak Season

15

The period of the year when travel demand, tourist numbers, and accommodation rates are highest.

Red-Eye Flight

16

A commercial flight scheduled to depart late at night and arrive early the next morning.

Eco-Tourism

17

Responsible travel to natural areas that conserves the environment and improves local well-being.

Carbon Footprint

18

The total amount of greenhouse gases generated directly and indirectly by an individual's travel activities.
